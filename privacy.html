About the app · Privacy · Use terms

Privacy notice

This notice describes the personal GND Archive Qualification integration. The operator and intended user is the owner of the connected Google account. Contact the operator at the address below about access, retention or deletion.

Information used

The app uses the authorized account's email identity, app-authorized Drive file and folder identifiers, names, sizes, permissions and checksums. Depending on the chosen job, it also reads and writes file contents, archive snapshots, manifests, checkpoints and task outputs. The current permission is drive.file, with email identity; it is not unrestricted Drive access.

Where information goes

Authorized files are stored in the connected account's Google Drive. Google-managed agent workspaces download and process the files required for a job. OAuth refresh material is held in a private credential vault; the runtime uses a credential reference to obtain authorized access. Credentials are not intended to appear in model prompts, ordinary logs, reports or public pages.

AI models may process task instructions, file metadata, selected file contents and tool results as part of the work. If the owner authorizes another AI provider or collaborator to review a job, the selected evidence or content shared for that review may go to that provider. The Drive grant itself does not authorize unrelated sharing. Provider processing and retention are also governed by the applicable provider account settings and terms.

Use, sharing and retention

The app uses this information to carry out the owner's requested storage, recovery, testing and agent tasks. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. It does not intentionally make Drive files public. Any sharing change requires an owner-authorized action.

Drive archives and ordinary files remain until the owner deletes them or a job with an explicit cleanup instruction removes them. Working copies, logs, model conversations and evidence receipts can remain in their respective workspaces or services under those services' retention settings. There is no app-wide automatic deletion period currently configured. Deleting an archive does not necessarily delete copies already downloaded into another workspace or a provider's retained conversation.

Your controls

You can stop jobs, delete app-created files in Drive, change their sharing, and remove this app's connection in your Google Account. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not itself delete stored files or earlier working copies. You can request cleanup of retained app evidence through the contact below. Keep independent backups of important work.

Google API data is handled for the user-facing functions described here, subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its applicable Limited Use requirements.
