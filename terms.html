About the app · Privacy · Use terms

Use terms

GND Archive Qualification is a personal experimental tool operated for the owner of its connected Google account. These terms describe its intended use and limits; it is not offered as a general public service.

Authorized use

Use the tool only with accounts, files and jobs you are entitled to authorize. Before starting a job, choose the content it may access, whether it may change or delete files, and which AI providers or collaborators may receive task data. Do not place passwords, API keys or other secrets in ordinary archives or model prompts unless the specific workflow has an appropriate secret-handling mechanism.

Storage and recovery

Jobs may create and modify ordinary Drive files or archive snapshots and restore them into a cloud workspace. Storage, transfer, file-size and API limits still apply. A successful checksum verifies the downloaded bytes for that test; it does not guarantee every future restore or prevent account access from expiring or being revoked.

The integration is under development. Jobs may stop, fail, overwrite an explicitly selected file, or require renewed Google authorization. Keep separate backups and review a job's permissions before allowing destructive changes. The tool does not promise continuous availability, a fixed performance level or unlimited storage or transfers.

Stopping use

You can stop running jobs and revoke the app's Google Account connection. Remove stored files and retained working copies separately when appropriate. The privacy notice explains the current data flows, retention and account controls.

Changes

Update these pages when the app's functions, requested scopes, data recipients or retention practices change. The date below identifies the current version of this notice.
